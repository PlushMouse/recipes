Sushi Rice

Description

Simple sushi rice, perfect for rolling sushi.

Ingredients

  • 2 1/4 Cups Short Grain White Rice
  • 2 1/4 Cups Water
  • 6 Tbsp Seasoned Rice Vinegar
  • 3 Tbsp Sugar
  • 4 Tsp Salt

Recipe

  1. Rinse rice with cool water and strain until water is mostly clear.
  2. Add rice and water to pot and cover, boil over high heat.
  3. After rice comes to a boil bring down to a simmer and cook for 15 minutes.
  4. Take off heat, let it sit covered for 10 minutes.
  5. Mix rice vinegar, sugar, and salt in a small bowl.
  6. Wet the inside of a large bowl, then add rice and vinegar mix. Carefully fold vinegar into rice.
  7. Cover with a damp towel and let cool to body temperature before using for sushi.
