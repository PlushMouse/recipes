Baked Pompano

Home

Description

A simple baked pompano recipe, perfect for preparing your fish to be used in a sushi roll.

Ingredients

  • 2 Pompano Fillets
  • 2 Tbsp Butter
  • 1 Tsp Lemon Juice
  • 1 Scallion, sliced

Recipe

  1. Preheat oven to 400° F.
  2. Melt butter in a small bowl and add lemon juice.
  3. Lay fillets in baking pan and score.
  4. Brush lemon butter over scored fish, then sprinkle scallions on top.
  5. Bake for 20 min, the internal temperature should reach 145° F.
