Pompano Sushi

Home

Description

A simple sushi recipe made with pompano, although you could substitute it for other kinds of fish. This particular recipe is one that I threw together based on a few others after my partner caught a few pompano and we wanted to try making something new with it.

I like to mix and match toppings on my rolls, so feel free to leave out any you don't like. Or add something you think sounds good!

Ingredients

  • Sushi Rice
  • Baked Pompano
  • Nori Sheets
  • 1 Avocado, sliced longways
  • 1 Cucumber, peeled and sliced into thin sticks
  • 3 Scallions, stalks seperated
  • Cream Cheese

Recipe

  1. Place the nori sheet rough side up on the bamboo mat. Wet fingers and evenly spread rice on top of the nori, leaving an empty edge for rolling later.
  2. Place desired toppings (Baked Pompano, Avocado, Cucumber, Scallions, and/or Cream Cheese) a few inches from the bottom edge of the nori sheet, opposite of the area we left clear for rolling.
  3. Start rolling your sushi by lifting the edge of the bamboo mat and rolling it away from you. Carefully tuck ingredients in as you roll if necessary.
  4. When you are almost done rolling, wet the edge of the nori then finish. This ensures it sticks to itself, completing your roll.
  5. Unroll the mat and move the sushi roll to a cutting board. First cut the roll in half, then line the two pieces up and cut into four pieces.
  6. Repeat with the remaining rolls then serve.
